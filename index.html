Privacy policy

Last updated
2026-11-10

This policy applies to the Ceramic Breaker iPhone and iPad game, published by Ceramic Breaker, and this information page. For privacy questions or requests, contact loqwallet@gmail.com.

Your progress stays with you

The game stores your level progress, stars, coins, energy, unlocked items, settings, tutorial history and Workshop drafts locally. It has no player account system and does not upload your saved progress to a game server. Your device backup service may include app data according to your device settings.

If iCloud is turned on for your Apple Account, the game also keeps a copy of your saved game progress in your own iCloud storage, so it can be restored on your other iPhone or iPad devices. Apple stores and syncs this copy under Apple's Privacy Policy; we do not receive it and cannot see it. Workshop drafts stay on the device where they were made.

Optional ads & privacy choices

You can choose to watch a rewarded ad to receive an in-game reward. Google AdMob provides these ads. Consent checks, SDK initialization and ad preparation can communicate with Google before an ad is displayed.

Google's ad services may process your IP address and approximate location, device or app identifiers, ad views and interactions, and crash, diagnostic and performance information to provide, measure and improve advertising. Access to Apple's advertising identifier requires tracking permission. The game's lack of player accounts does not prevent Google from processing identifiers under its own policies.

Where required, Google's consent form lets you make advertising choices. You can reopen available ad privacy options from the Store. If iOS asks for tracking permission, you may decline; the game does not require tracking permission to play or to qualify for an optional ad reward. Ad availability still depends on consent choices, network access and available ads.

You can manage tracking permission in iOS Settings → Privacy & Security → Tracking. Learn more in Google's Privacy Policy and how Google uses information from apps that use its services.

Purchases

Apple processes in-app payments. We do not receive your payment-card details. The game uses verified purchase information to grant items and restore eligible permanent purchases. Apple handles purchase information under Apple's Privacy Policy.

Reminders

If you allow notifications, the game schedules local reminders on your device, such as daily rewards and energy reminders. These are not remote push messages from a game server. You can disable notifications in your device's Settings.

Contacting support & visiting this page

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to send. We use it to answer your request and keep it only as needed to handle support and any applicable legal obligations. Please do not send passwords, payment-card details or other unnecessary sensitive information.

This page uses no advertising, analytics scripts, cookies or forms of its own, and loads no external fonts. GitHub Pages hosts it and may process technical visitor information, including IP addresses, for security and service operation. See the GitHub General Privacy Statement. Your email provider and ours also process messages under their own policies.

Your choices & deleting data

Open the game's Settings → Delete my data to erase its local saved data and its iCloud copy. This cannot be undone. Deleting your data does not delete Apple's purchase records, device backups, or information held by Google. Those are managed through their respective services and privacy controls. Removing the app normally removes its local files, but device backups and the iCloud copy remain until you use Delete my data.

The game does not access your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location. To ask about support messages we hold, request their deletion, or exercise applicable privacy rights, email loqwallet@gmail.com. We can act on information under our control; for information processed by Apple or Google, use their linked policies and controls. Where applicable, you may also contact your local data-protection authority.

Children & policy updates

The game is not directed to children under 13. If you believe a child has provided personal information to us, contact us so we can address it. We may update this policy as the game changes; the date above identifies the latest revision.

Reporting a problem

Please include your game version, level number, device model, iOS or iPadOS version, and a short description of what happened. A screenshot can help. Keep the app installed while we troubleshoot, so your progress stays available.

A permanent purchase is missing

Open the Store and use Restore Purchases with the same Apple Account that made the purchase. Eligible permanent purchases can be restored; consumable coins and energy are not restored by this button. If a problem remains, email the item name and what happened. Do not send payment-card details.

A rewarded ad did not play

Check your connection and try again later. Ads may be unavailable because of inventory, consent choices or connection problems. Rewards are granted after the game receives the ad's completion confirmation. You do not need to allow tracking to play.

I want to manage notifications

Open your device's Settings → Notifications → Ceramic Breaker to change notification permission.

My progress is missing on a new device

Sign in to the same Apple Account on both devices, make sure iCloud is turned on, and open the game while connected to the internet. Your progress is restored automatically. If both devices have different progress, the game asks which one to keep; the other stays on that device as a backup.

I want to delete my progress

Use Settings → Delete my data in the game. This removes local progress and settings, and the iCloud copy of your progress. Device backups and Apple's purchase history are managed separately. Contact support before deleting if you are trying to recover a save.
